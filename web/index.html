Hand-Gesture Portal Filter

Spread both hands and frame the portal with your thumbs and index fingers.

How to use

  1. Allow your camera. Wait for hand tracking to load. Keep both hands visible and use good lighting.
  2. Make a frame. Hold up both hands with your thumbs and index fingers extended, then spread your hands apart. These four fingertips form the corners of the portal.
  3. Move the corners. Move your thumbs and index fingers to resize and reshape the filtered area. Bring your hands together or lower them to close it.
  4. Change the filter. Touch your thumb to your pinky on either hand, then release before pinching again. You can also use the Prev filter and Next filter buttons.
  5. Save a picture. Click Screenshot to download the current view as a PNG.
